About

Dr. Khandaker Iftekharul Islam is a Postdoctoral Scholar at the University of California, Santa Cruz, where he conducts research on equitable and climate-resilient water allocation frameworks as part of the interdisciplinary COEQWAL project. His work integrates water system modeling, machine learning, remote sensing, and GIS to address critical challenges in watershed hydrology, water policy, and environmental justice.


Dr. Islam earned his PhD in Water Science and Management (Water Informatics concentration) from New Mexico State University in 2021, receiving the Dean's Award of Excellence upon graduation. He holds an MSc in Civil and Environmental Engineering (Geo and Water Resources) from Chalmers University of Technology, Sweden, and a BSc in Civil Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET), Bangladesh.


Prior to his current role, Dr. Islam served as a Project Manager at the New Mexico Environment Department (SWQB), a Research Associate at the USDA Southwest Climate Hub, and as a faculty member (Lecturer and Assistant Professor) in the Department of Civil Engineering at IUBAT University, Bangladesh. He has also served as a consultant for CEGIS – the Center for Environment and Geographic Information Services under the Ministry of Water Resources, Bangladesh.


Dr. Islam has presented his research at international venues including the AGU Fall Meeting, Bay-Delta Science Conference, California Water Data Summit, and the Western Snow Conference. He is an invited reviewer for several leading journals including Journal of Hydrology, Environmental Modelling & Software, and Remote Sensing.
